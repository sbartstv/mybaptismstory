Privacy

My Baptism Story is built so your story stays yours. This site does not upload, collect or store your story, your video or your personal details.

The short version. Everything you type or record on this site stays in your browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. Your story only leaves your device when you choose to share it yourself, through an app like Facebook, Instagram or YouTube.

What this site does not do

  • It does not upload your video. Videos you record or choose stay on your device until you download or share them.
  • It does not save the written story, name, city or link you enter in Add yours. They show on your screen only and disappear when you close or reload the page.
  • It does not save the parent or guardian name and email entered on the consent step. They are used only on your device to unlock the next step.
  • It does not use cookies, accounts, logins, advertising or analytics tracking.
  • It does not sell or share personal information, because it does not collect any.

Services that load when you visit

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical data such as your IP address when the page loads. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this page, so your browser requests them from Google. See the Google Privacy Policy.

When you share

The Share and Connect buttons open other apps and sites, such as Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and baptize.org. Once you post there, that service's privacy policy applies, not this one. You stay in control of what you post and can delete it there at any time.

Checking "Yes, Baptize The World may share my story" in Add yours is your permission for Baptize The World to share a story you send them. In this version of the site, that choice is not sent anywhere on its own.

Children and teens

Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian to give permission before recording or sharing a story. See the Parental Consent page. This site does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Sample stories

The stories shown on the home page are samples written for this demo. They are not real people. Real stories will appear only with the person's consent.

Changes

If this site ever starts collecting or storing information, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change.
