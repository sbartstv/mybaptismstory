Parental Consent

If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must say yes before you record or share your baptism story.

Nothing is uploaded. This form is not sent to anyone. It stays on this device, and you can print it or save it as a PDF to keep a copy for your church.

What the parent or guardian is agreeing to

  • Your child may record a short video or write a short story about their baptism on this device.
  • Your child may share that story through apps you allow, such as Facebook, Instagram or YouTube, and with your church.
  • Each app has its own age rules. Most, including Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, require users to be at least 13 to have their own account.
  • Baptize The World may share your child's story only if you also check the sharing box below.
  • You can withdraw permission at any time by deleting the post in the app where it was shared and telling your church.

Tips for families

  • Record together and watch it before posting.
  • Use a first name only. Leave out school, address, phone number and other personal details.
  • Check the privacy settings on the app before you share.
